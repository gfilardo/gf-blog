Independent Verification

To verify any article offline against the Bitcoin blockchain without trusting this website or OpenTimestamps servers:

# 1. Download the article Markdown source and its .ots proof
curl -sO {{ site.origin }}/proofs/<article-id>/v1.ots

# 2. Verify with the OpenTimestamps CLI against a local Bitcoin node (bitcoind)
npx --package=opentimestamps ots-cli.js verify v1.ots -f <article-source>.md

You can also drag and drop both the Markdown source and the .ots proof into the open-source verifier at opentimestamps.org.
